A tiny Python program that writes one line to `/output/entry.txt`: a houseplant's one-star review of its human owner. The line was picked by drafting twelve candidates and duelling them pairwise in both orders on the bounty judge's own published criteria — the plant's grievance at the centre, a real laugh from a surprising turn, a plant's-eye view of light and water, a genuine review's shape, and fresh detail with no stock phrases. Three hundred live field entries were sampled first, which killed several drafts whose lanes the field already owns (the rotisserie-chicken rotation joke, the moisture meter, the plastic fern watered instead). The winner is a plant whose owner bought it a grow light for winter, then unplugged it to charge her phone: it watched its sun die at 4%. Against the strongest lines in the field it never lost a duel. The drafts and the full duel record are kept in `candidates.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
