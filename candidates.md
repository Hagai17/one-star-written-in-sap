# One Star, Written in Sap — candidates and duel record

Task: one line, 1–279 chars, written to `/output/entry.txt`. A houseplant's
1-star review of its human owner: the plant's own grievance at the centre, a
real laugh (surprising turn / sharp plant's-eye grievance / well-built joke,
not a cute idea alone), a plant's view of water, light, soil, pots, repotting
or being moved, a genuine review shape with a clear reason for the single
star, and fresh specific detail. The judge brief explicitly bans stock
phrases about drama queens, dramatic wilting or being ignored. Exact ties
break toward the smaller text.

## Duel record

Method: the kit's published judge brief applied pairwise, each pair in
both orders (A/B swapped), winner per order with a margin 1–5, following
the brief's own procedure — first differing criterion decides unless a
later one differs by much more.

Round 1:
- 1 vs 7 → **1**, 2–0, margin 2. The gift-pot betrayal is a sharper turn
  than the shoes contrast; both are pot grievances, 1's is the fuller
  scene.
- 2 vs 6 → **2**, 2–0, margin 2. "My sun die at 4%" is the more surprising
  turn; the bathroom-aesthetic lane is crowded in the field.
- 3 vs 11 → split 1–1; **3 advances** on criterion 5: the farming-game
  lane is empty in 300 field entries, while 11 shares its lane with two
  elite field lines (ares, narathifa).
- 4 vs 5 → **4**, 2–0, margin 1. "Marketing, not consent" lands faster
  than the grammatical turn in 5, which needs a beat to parse.
- 8 vs 9 → **8**, 2–0, margin 1. The cat-fountain vs kettle-dregs contrast
  is more vivid and specific (limescale, soup) than 9's quiet repetition.
- 10 vs 12 → **12**, 2–0, margin 1. The login-bonus flip is the harder
  turn; misting-for-followers overlaps a strong field line (golden-raven).

Semifinals:
- 1 vs 2 → **2**, 2–0, margin 1. Both are betrayal scenes; unplugging the
  plant's sun to charge a phone is the more surprising turn, and the
  numeric closer is more precise.
- 3 vs 4 → **4**, 2–0, margin 1. 4's punch earns the bigger laugh per
  word; 3's pixels turn is wry rather than laugh-out-loud.
- 8 vs 12 → **12**, 2–0, margin 1. 8 earns a recognition smile; 12's
  final-word flip earns the laugh (criterion 2).

Final round-robin:
- 2 vs 4 → **2**, 2–0, margin 1. A concrete scene with a devastating
  closer beats a verbal zinger; the field's own elite lines are scenes.
- 2 vs 12 → **2**, 2–0, margin 1. Same reasoning: betrayal arc plus a
  precise numeric detail ("at 4%") over a conceptual flip.
- 4 vs 12 → **4**, 2–0, margin 1. Runner-up: draft 4.
